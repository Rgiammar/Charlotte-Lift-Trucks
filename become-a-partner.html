The Process
How the Match Works

Five steps from customer inquiry to lead delivery. The inventory confirmation step is what separates our model from a standard lead aggregator. You only pay for leads you can actually fulfill.

01

Customer Inquiry

A business in the Charlotte metro submits a request through our site or calls our matching desk with a specific equipment need.

02

We Qualify

Our team contacts the customer within 1 business hour to verify the inquiry is legitimate, the business is reachable, and the equipment need is real.

03

Inventory Confirmed

Before routing the lead, we confirm you have matching inventory available for that customer's specific need. No match means no lead and no fee.

04

Lead Delivered

You receive the customer's full contact details, equipment need, budget, timeline, and facility info. One provider receives this lead on our platform.

05

You Close It

You contact the customer directly and handle the transaction your way. A flat referral fee is due at the time we deliver the lead.

Ideal Partners
Who We Are Looking For

We work best with independent local forklift providers who have the inventory, bandwidth, and responsiveness to follow up on leads quickly and serve Charlotte area customers well.

  • Independent forklift dealers, rental companies, or leasing operations
  • Serving the Charlotte metro, Greenville-Spartanburg, or surrounding counties
  • Able to respond to leads the same business day. Customers move fast
  • Offering at least one of: sales, rental, or leasing
  • Willing to confirm inventory availability before leads are routed to you
  • Licensed and in good standing in your state of operation
  • Committed to a direct, professional customer experience

Not the Right Fit If...

You are a national chain or large regional operation with a centralized call center. Our model is built around independent local providers who give customers a direct, personal experience.

You cannot respond to leads the same business day. Leads go cold fast and we want to match customers with providers who will follow up immediately.

You are not willing to confirm inventory before handoff. The inventory confirmation step is a core part of our matching process and protects both parties from wasted time and money.
